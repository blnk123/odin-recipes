Lasagna

Make a classic, comforting Homemade Lasagna in about 1 hour and 30 minutes with simple meat Sauce, ricotta cheese mixture, and tender noodles.

Ingredients

  • Meat Sauce:
    • 1/2 lb. ground beef and 1/2 lb. ground Italian sausage (or pork)
    • 1 small onion (minced) and 1-2 cloves garlic (minced)
    • 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes and 1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
    • 1 tsp Italian seasoning, 1.2 tsp dried oregano, 2 tbsp fresh parsley, pinch of sugar, saly and black pepper
  • Cheese Filling:
    • 15-16 oz ricotta cheese (or small-cur cottage cheese)
    • 3/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
    • 1 large egg
    • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Assembly:
    • 12 lasagna noodles (boil per package or use no-boil noodles)
    • 16 oz shredded mozzarella cheese (divided)

Steps

  1. Make the Meat Sauce:
    2.
    • Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat and brown the ground beef and sausage.
    • Add the onion and garlic, cooking until soft (about 5 minutes).
    • Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, herbs, sugar, salt, and pepper. Simmer on medium-low for 30 minutes.
  2. Boil the Noodles:
    3.
    • Cook the lasagna noodles in a large pot of boiling salted water until al dente (8 to 10 minutes), then drain.
  3. Mix the Cheese:
    4.
    • In a large bowl, combine the ricotta (or cottage cheese), Parmesan cheese, egg, parsley, salt, and peper.
  4. Layer the Lasagna:
    5.
    • Pre heat your oven to 375 degrees Fahrenheit (190 degrees celcius).
    • Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish.
    • Add a layer of lasgna noodles.
    • Spread half of the cheese mixture over the noodles, add a layer of meat sauce, and sprinke with mozzarella cheese.
    • Repeat the layers once more, ending with a final layer of noodles topped with remaining sauce and mozzarella cheese.
  5. Bake and Serve:
    6.
    • Cover the dish with aluminum foil and bake for 30 to 40 minutes.
    • Remover the foil and bake for another 5 to 10 minutes until the cheese is golden and bubbly.
    • Let it rest for 10 to 15 minutes before slicing and serving.
